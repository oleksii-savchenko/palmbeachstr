Full-Service STR Design

01

Interior Design

Full-service interior design tailored to short-term rentals. Julia creates spaces that feel like a luxury escape — and perform like a business. From initial concept and mood board to material selection, floor plans, and final installation oversight, every decision is made with your guest experience and nightly rate in mind.

02

Furnishing & Styling

We handle every piece — from sourcing and procurement to delivery, assembly, and final placement. Artwork, cushions, throws, plants, kitchenware, towels, even the welcome basket. You hand us the keys and receive a fully furnished, styled, guest-ready property. No chasing vendors, no coordinating deliveries.

03

Space Organization

Curated, functional spaces designed for real life. From closet systems and kitchen organization to full move-in setup — we create order that lasts and spaces that actually work.

04

Photography Ready

Great spaces deserve great photos. We stage every property — rental, vacation home, or primary residence — specifically for the camera. Optimal angles, hero shots, and the styling details that make viewers stop scrolling and start booking.

05

Revenue Strategy

Every design choice is an investment decision. Julia analyses comparable STR properties in your market, identifies what top-performing listings have in common, and reverse-engineers that into your space. Amenity selection, bedroom count optimisation, dedicated workspace, outdoor staging — each feature is chosen to maximise ADR (average daily rate) and occupancy.
